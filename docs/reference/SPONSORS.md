# Sponsor policy

QFF is free and stays free: the site, the data (CC BY 4.0) and the MCP server. Support pays for upkeep (the hourly
scan, review passes, new models). It never buys influence.

- **Sponsors never change the data.** Values, sets, ordering, the efficiency frontier and "Beaten by" are all computed by
  code from publishers' own model cards. No sponsor can add, remove, reorder or hide a claim.
- **No paid placements and no ads.** There are no featured slots, badges or banners for sale.
- **Disclosure.** A sponsor that also publishes quants is listed below, and its repos carry a "sponsor" label on the site.
- **Corrections are the same for everyone.** Sponsor or not, a fix needs the card line that shows it, and the gates
  re-check it.
- **Payments happen off-site** (Ko-fi / PayPal). The site only links out and never takes payments or personal data.
